About us

An independent IT consultancy

CCM CONSULTANCY LTD provides information technology consultancy. The company was incorporated on 20 May 2011 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 07642172.

We work with businesses and organisations that need independent IT expertise: help choosing the right technology, planning and delivering change, or taking an honest look at the systems and processes already in place.

As a director-led consultancy, you deal with one accountable point of contact from the first conversation onwards. Our aim is simple: clear, practical advice in plain English that you can act on.

Services

How we can help

Every engagement is shaped around what your organisation needs. These are the main areas we cover.

IT advisory

Independent advice on technology choices, systems and roadmaps, so decisions are made with a clear view of the options, costs and trade-offs.

  • Technology options and selection
  • IT strategy and roadmap planning
  • A second opinion on proposals and plans

Project and delivery support

Hands-on help planning and delivering IT change, from shaping the scope at the start to keeping the work on track through to completion.

  • Scoping, planning and requirements
  • Coordinating suppliers and stakeholders
  • Support through implementation and handover

Systems review

An objective assessment of your current systems and processes, with clear recommendations on what to keep, what to improve and where to focus first.

  • Review of systems, tools and workflows
  • Identifying gaps, risks and duplication
  • Prioritised, practical recommendations

Ongoing consultancy

Flexible, engagement-based support for in-house teams, for when you need an independent outside view on a regular basis rather than a one-off project.

  • Support for in-house IT and project teams
  • Advice as questions and decisions come up
  • Scope agreed to suit each engagement
